# WorkLingo

A simulated Slack workplace where a learner practises Business English by replying to AI colleagues (Personas) under time pressure.

## Language

**Persona**:
An AI colleague with a fixed role and personality who chats with the learner.

**Task**:
A piece of work a Persona assigns to the learner through a Slack message, which the learner must answer in writing before a Deadline.
_Avoid_: Mission, nhiệm vụ, assignment

**Talking point**:
One specific piece of content the learner must get across when answering a Task, written in Vietnamese so the learner has to phrase it in English themselves. A Task has 1–3 Talking points.
_Avoid_: Checklist item, key message, required point

**Work context**:
The fictional project situation generated once per session (project, the feature the learner is building, progress, blockers) that every Talking point draws from, so facts stay consistent within a session.
_Avoid_: Scenario, backstory

**Deadline**:
The time limit for the learner to reply to a Persona's request; missing it produces a Warning.

**Warning**:
A penalty recorded when the learner leaves a request unanswered past its Deadline (ghosting).
